# Phase 1 — CRM

## Goal

Replace WhatsApp-based sales memory with a traceable lead-to-client workflow.

## Deliverables

- Users/team view needed for assignment.
- Leads with canonical stages, owners, sources, interests, values, next follow-up, and stage history.
- Pipeline Kanban and summary metrics.
- Meetings with attendees, structured completion, decisions, requirements, and next action.
- Follow-ups with overdue/today/upcoming views.
- Clients and contacts, including finance/technical contact flags.
- Lead conversion to client and optional project handoff.
- Notes, proposals/quotation metadata and versioned files, attachments, and CRM timelines.

## Acceptance checks

- A lead can progress through stages and every transition is preserved.
- A completed meeting can create an assigned follow-up/task.
- A won lead preserves history while creating/reusing a client.
- Unauthorized users cannot access CRM records through direct requests.
